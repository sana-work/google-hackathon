🔴 CRITICAL
Adversarial Scenario That Exposed Gap
"Electronics item delivered exactly 14 days ago, but order placed 17 days ago — am I eligible for return?"
Root Cause
Policy text defines eligibility by delivery date, but the compiled flow binds DaysSinceDelivery to order timestamp, producing incorrect decisions at the 14‑day boundary.
✅ Required Fix: Bind DaysSinceDelivery to the carrier tracking API delivery confirmation timestamp (not order timestamp). Add a validation guard and re-run fuzz suite. Paths affected: 23
