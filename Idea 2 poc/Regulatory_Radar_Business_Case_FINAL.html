The Reality: Enterprise compliance teams are overwhelmed. A single DPDPA amendment can affect 7+ internal policies, 14+ client DPA contracts, and 3+ technology systems. Manual review takes 3-4 weeks per regulation — but regulators give 30-60 day compliance windows. Firms face a structural impossible task.

🏦 BFSI Client Example — Current State Pain

A mid-size private bank we interviewed receives ~2 regulatory circulars per week from RBI, SEBI, and DPDPA authorities. Their 6-person compliance team manually reviews each, cross-references it against 850+ policy documents, and then manually updates downstream processes. Each major regulatory change consumes 3-4 weeks of compliance team capacity — leaving virtually no bandwidth for proactive risk management.

The Solution
Regulatory Radar — 4.2 Minutes vs. 4 Weeks

Regulatory Radar uses a multi-agent Gemini architecture (prototype demonstrated using synthetic circulars and non-confidential policy samples) to monitor regulatory sources (configurable feeds) (MCA, RBI, SEBI, MHA gazette), automatically parse new circulars, and map changes to specific internal policy gaps — in minutes, not weeks.

Core Value Proposition: Every regulatory change triggers an automatic, structured Impact Assessment Report listing: (1) which internal policies are affected, (2) what specifically must change, (3) who the responsible owner is, and (4) how many days until compliance deadline — all with document-grounded citations an auditor can verify.

What’s Novel (Why this wins): Regulatory Radar is not a “regulation summarizer.” It produces audit-ready Impact Cards that (1) map each clause change to specific internal policies / contracts / systems, (2) attach verifiable citations, (3) assign severity + deadline countdown, and (4) generate a remediation action plan with owners — enabling compliance operations at enterprise scale.
