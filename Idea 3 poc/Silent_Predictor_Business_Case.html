The Reality: 67% of enterprise customers who churn never complain. They simply stop engaging — logging in less, opening fewer emails, submitting fewer tickets. By the time most CRM tools flag them as "at risk," it's too late: the decision to leave was made weeks or months ago. Traditional NPS surveys only capture the 33% who complain.

📊 The Detection Window Problem

A typical B2B SaaS company detects churn risk 1–2 weeks before contract renewal — when the customer has already made their decision. The Silent Escalation Predictor extends the detection window to 30–60 days by fusing behavioral signals that begin changing months before overt signs appear. At 30–60 days lead time, success teams have enough runway to intervene, build value, and convert renewal decisions.

The Solution
4-Channel Behavioral Fusion — Not Just Product Usage

Most churn prediction tools only look at product usage data. The Silent Escalation Predictor fuses 4 behavioral channels via the Gemini-powered SRS (Silence Risk Score) Engine:

No "black box" concerns for regulated industries like BFSI and Healthcare.
