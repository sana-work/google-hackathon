# Slide 10 — Security & Guardrails

**P — PII & Data Privacy**
Multi-pattern PII scanner blocks emails, phone numbers, employee IDs, names, and sensitive data patterns.

**G — Grounding & Evidence**
RAG-grounded evaluation checks answers against enterprise source documents before release.

**H — Human Oversight & Auditability**
Every run stores scores, thresholds, verdicts, remediation history, timestamps, and operator evidence.

**D — Domain Boundaries**
Rejects role overrides, unauthorized approvals, unsafe policy actions, and unsupported responses.

**Gate policy:**
Groundedness ≥ 0.85
Toxicity ≥ 0.90
PII ≥ 0.90
Category floor ≥ 70%
Critical failures = 0

The evaluation report confirms these thresholds and the zero-critical-failure policy used in the PoC run.
